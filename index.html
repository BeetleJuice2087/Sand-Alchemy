Settings

✨ Cauldron spirit (AI)

When two things have no recipe, the Cauldron spirit can invent something brand new: an element or a creature. Inventions are saved, and you can mix them again.

This browser can’t run AI on its graphics chip (it needs WebGPU), so only Online is available here.

It needs about 2 GB of memory while it thinks, so it's for computers only, unless your phone or tablet has plenty of memory. On a phone, pick Small and quick or use Online.
